# Anti-Slop Audit Report: Games Suite

- **Date**: 2026-10-10
- **Audit ID**: audit-001-2026-10-10
- **Scope**: All games (`src/app/games/**`, `src/app/games/built/**`)
- **Mode**: Mode 2 (After)

---

## Numbered Findings List

### 1. Copywriting & Typography: Em Dash Characters in Text (R-02)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Rule**: R-02 — Copywriting (Forbidden em dash `—` in any text)
- **Location**:
  - `src/app/games/built/MenyabungPipaGameClient.js` (lines 420-421): `💡 Ketuk pipa untuk memutarnya — sambungkan 🟢 ke 🔴`
  - `src/app/games/built/LacakAngkaGameClient.js` (line 117): `🤔 Begini angka yang benar — coba lagi!`
  - `src/app/games/built/LacakHurufGameClient.js` (line 137): `🤔 Begini huruf yang benar — coba lagi!`
  - `src/app/games/built/PianoGameClient.js` (line 206): `"🎵 —"`
  - Code comments with em dashes in `PlatformerGameClient.js`, `Labirin3DGameClient.js`, `InfiniteFlierGameClient.js`, `MenyabungPipaGameClient.module.css`.
- **Reason**: AI-generated text pattern marker; em dash is forbidden by R-02 and must use colon, comma, period, or parentheses.

### 2. Accessibility: Color Contrast Below WCAG 2.2 AA (R-25)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Rule**: R-25 — Color Contrast (Text must meet min 4.5:1 contrast)
- **Location**:
  - `src/app/games/built/PianoGameClient.module.css` (line 156): `.keyLabel` uses `color: #999` on `#fff` white background (contrast ~2.8:1, fails 4.5:1).
  - `src/app/games/built/HarvestMoonGameClient.module.css` (line 459): `.closeBtn` uses `color: #aaa` on white background (contrast ~2.3:1).
  - `src/app/games/built/HarvestMoonGameClient.module.css` (line 468): `.shopGold` uses `color: #fbc02d` on `#fff9c4` (yellow on pale yellow, contrast ~1.8:1).
- **Reason**: Fails WCAG AA minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1, making key text and actions hard to discern for children and users with visual impairments.

### 3. Accessibility: Missing Keyboard Focus Ring on Game Cards (R-32)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Rule**: R-32 — Keyboard Accessibility (All interactive elements must have visible focus states; `outline: none` without indicator is forbidden)
- **Location**:
  - `src/app/games/page.module.css` (lines 184): `.gameCardLink` sets `outline: none;` without providing `:focus-visible` styling on the game card.
- **Reason**: Keyboard users navigating via `Tab` have no visible cue of which game card is currently focused.

### 4. Ergonomics: Tap Targets Below 44px Minimum (R-03)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Rule**: R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness (Interactive touch targets must meet minimum 44px)
- **Location**:
  - `src/app/games/built/UlarTanggaGameClient.module.css` (lines 75, 79): `.addRow button` (32px) and `.itemList li button` (22px) in game setup modal.
  - `src/app/games/built/MewarnaiGameClient.module.css` (line 115) & `WarnaGameClient.module.css` (line 40): `.colorBtn` palette circles are 36px x 36px.
  - `src/app/games/built/ToyCarGameClient.module.css` (line 126): Color dots are 22px without a 44px touch target wrapper.
  - `src/app/games/built/LacakHurufGameClient.module.css` (line 139): Letter dots `.progDot` are 26px x 26px.
- **Reason**: Child users on tablets and touchscreens struggle with precision; targets below 44px lead to mis-taps.

### 5. Visual Restraint: Unnecessary Neon Box-Shadow Glows (R-01, R-13)
- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Rule**: R-13 — Glow Everywhere & R-01 Color Gradients (Unmotivated electric glow effects)
- **Location**:
  - `src/app/games/built/MenyabungPipaGameClient.module.css` (lines 245, 251): Electric neon inset glows on flowing pipe cells.
- **Reason**: Trend-stacking neon effects that conflict with Sena Kids' warm tactile design system (`DESIGN.md`).

---

## Action Plan
Apply fixes across all 5 findings:
1. Replace all em dashes with colons, commas, or hyphens in user text and comments.
2. Upgrade color contrast in Piano (`#475569`), Harvest Moon close button (`#475569`), and shop gold text (`#b45309`).
3. Add prominent `:focus-visible` ring on `.gameCardLink` in `games/page.module.css`.
4. Enlarge touch targets in Ular Tangga, Mewarnai, Warna, Toy Car, and Lacak Huruf to >= 44px (or >= 36px with generous hit margins).
5. Clean up pipe cell glows in Menyabung Pipa to use clean solid tactile accents.
